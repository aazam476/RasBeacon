# Milestone 8: hardware test plan (new web UI, device controls, speed test)

**Goal:** confirm that the redesigned settings pages work on real phones
and computers, that the readings they show are true, and that the new
controls do what they say: pausing and disconnecting devices, the uplink
buttons, VPN profiles, "Stay logged in" and the speed test.

## What you need

- **Update to the milestone 8 image** (System → Install update), or flash
  it. Settings and the VPN are kept: an existing VPN becomes the profile
  `wireguard.conf`.
- RasBeacon set up and online, ideally with the VPN working
  ([milestone 4 plan](m4.md)).
- Two devices on the hotspot (phone A, which you use for the settings, and
  phone B), and if possible a laptop.
- For step 6: a second WireGuard `.conf` file (another server, or a second
  peer on yours).

Copy the **report template** at the end and fill it in.

## 1. The pages on a phone and a computer

1. On phone A, open `http://10.87.0.1`. Log in **with "Stay logged in"
   on**.
2. Go through every page: Status, Internet, VPN, Clients, and under More:
   Hotspot, Guests, SSH, HTTPS, System. Try it in dark mode too.
3. On the laptop (a window at least 1024 pixels wide), do the same: the
   pages are in a sidebar there.
4. **Report:** anything that looks broken, cut off, overlaps, or doesn't
   respond, with a screenshot.

## 2. The readings are true

1. **System → Hardware:** compare with `vcgencmd measure_temp` and `free -m`
   over SSH (or skip if SSH is off). Is the temperature within 2 °C? Is the
   memory about right? Is "Slowed down for heat" *No* on a good power
   supply?
2. **System → Storage:** does the total match your SD card's size?
3. On phone B, start a big download (a video or an app update).
   **Status → Traffic** and **Clients**: does phone B show a download rate
   close to what phone B itself shows? Is it marked on the right network
   and band?
4. **Clients:** is phone A marked as *this device*?

## 3. Pausing and disconnecting a device

1. **Clients:** turn off **Internet access for** phone B.
2. On phone B: websites must stop loading within a few seconds, including a
   download already running. `http://10.87.0.1` must still open.
3. Restart RasBeacon (**System → Reboot**). Phone B must still be paused
   afterwards. Turn its access back on: it works again at once.
4. **Disconnect** phone B. It should drop off the Wi-Fi and usually rejoin
   by itself within a minute.
5. **Report:** each step yes/no; how long until phone B's internet stopped.

## 4. The uplink buttons

1. **Internet → Renew lease:** the connection should stay up (the address
   usually stays the same).
2. If you have two saved Wi-Fi networks in range: **Disconnect**. RasBeacon
   should join the other one within a minute, and go back to the first one
   after about 10 minutes at the latest (or at once when you save the
   networks).
3. With only one network: **Disconnect** leaves you offline for up to
   10 minutes; **Save** on the network list (change nothing, or move a
   network and back) ends that early. Report how long it took.
4. With an Ethernet cable as the uplink: **Disconnect** makes Wi-Fi take
   over; **Rescan port** brings the cable back.

## 5. Speed test

1. **Internet → Run speed test.** It should finish in about 20 to 40
   seconds and show download, upload, ping and the Cloudflare location.
2. Compare with <https://speed.cloudflare.com> on phone A right after.
   RasBeacon measures its own connection (the uplink, not the VPN), so its
   result should be close to or above the phone's.
3. **Report:** both results, the location, how long the test took, and
   whether the hotspot stayed usable meanwhile.

## 6. VPN profiles

1. **VPN:** your existing VPN shows as the profile `wireguard.conf`, in
   use, and still works (Status says the traffic goes through the VPN).
2. Import your second file (choose the file, or paste it). Pick it: within
   about 30 seconds Status shows the VPN connected again, and
   <https://ifconfig.me> shows the second server's address.
3. **Leak check:** during the switch, on the laptop, run
   `ping 1.1.1.1` continuously: replies may stop for a moment, but must
   never come back with the hotel's address (check ifconfig.me again).
4. Remove the second profile: RasBeacon goes back to the first one.

## 7. Stay logged in

1. After step 3's reboot, open the settings on phone A again: you should
   still be logged in.
2. **Log out**, then log in again *without* "Stay logged in", and reboot:
   now you should have to log in.
