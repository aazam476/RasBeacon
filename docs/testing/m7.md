# Milestone 7: hardware test plan (guest network, metered, IPv6, zero-leak login)

**Goal:** confirm that the guest network works and is cut off from your
devices, that Android sees the metered setting, that a routed IPv6
network reaches devices through the VPN, and that you can log in to a
hotel's login page from RasBeacon's own browser without anything leaving
the VPN.

## What you need

- **Flash the milestone 7 image.** Its system partitions are bigger, so
  older images can't update to it.
- RasBeacon set up and online, with the VPN working
  ([milestone 4 plan](m4.md)) and the kill switch on.
- Two phones (ideally one iPhone, one Android) or a phone and a laptop.
- For step 4: a network with a login page (hotel, café, library, campus
  guest network).
- Step 3 is optional: it needs a WireGuard server that routes a public
  IPv6 /64 to RasBeacon (most home setups don't have this; skip it if
  unsure).

Copy the **report template** at the end and fill it in.

## 1. Guest network

1. Open the **Guests** page.
   - If it says *This Wi-Fi adapter can only broadcast one network*,
     report that (with your adapter, e.g. Archer T3U) and skip to step 2.
     Then download **System → Diagnostics** and send the `radio:` lines
     and the `iw` sections.
2. Tick **Turn on the guest network**, keep or change the name, choose a
   guest password (different from your own), **Save**. Your devices
   disconnect for a few seconds.
3. Join the guest network from phone B.
4. **Report:**
   - does phone B get internet? Open <https://ifconfig.me>: is the address
     your VPN server's (not the hotel's)?
   - on phone B, open `http://10.87.0.1` and `http://10.87.1.1`: both must
     **fail** (no settings page).
   - from phone B, can you see phone A (on your own network) in AirDrop,
     Nearby Share, printer lists, or by pinging its address (shown on the
     Status page)? Must be **no**.
   - Status page: does *Guest network* show *Running* and 1 guest?
5. Check that guests follow the VPN: on the **VPN** page, untick
   **Use the VPN**, save, then tick it again and save while watching phone
   B. **Report:** does phone B lose internet briefly and come back through
   the VPN (check <https://ifconfig.me> again)?
6. Leave the guest network on or off as you like.

## 2. Metered connection

1. **Hotspot** page → tick **Metered connection** → **Save**. The page
   should say *Saved.* and your devices should **not** disconnect.
2. On the Android phone: turn Wi-Fi off and on (it reads the setting when it
   joins). Then *Settings → Network & internet → Internet →* RasBeacon's
   network → *Network usage* (wording varies).
3. **Report:** does Android show *Treat as metered* / *Detect automatically*
   resolved to metered? (On some versions: *Data usage → metered*.)
4. Untick it again and save.

## 3. Public IPv6 through the VPN (optional)

1. Your WireGuard server must route a /64 to RasBeacon (its peer entry for
   RasBeacon lists that /64 in `AllowedIPs`), and the WireGuard file must
   give RasBeacon an IPv6 address and `::/0` in `AllowedIPs`.
2. **VPN** page → *Server details* → enter the /64 (like
   `2001:db8:1234:5678::/64`) → **Save**.
3. On a phone on the hotspot, open <https://test-ipv6.com>.
4. **Report:** the IPv6 address shown (it should start with your /64), and
   the score. The Status page should say *Public addresses through the VPN*.

## 4. Zero-leak login on a hotel network

1. With the VPN on and the kill switch on, connect RasBeacon to the network
   with the login page (as in the [milestone 5 plan](m5.md)).
2. On your phone, wait for *This network needs a login* (or open
   `http://10.87.0.1/#portal`).
3. Tap **Open the login page here**. Within about 10 seconds, the hotel's
   login page should appear as a picture.
4. Log in:
   - tap fields and buttons on the picture;
   - to type, tap the field on the picture, type into the box under it,
     tap **Type it**;
   - **Enter**, **Delete**, **Next field**, **Back** and **Reload** do what
     they say; swipe on the picture to scroll.
5. After logging in, within about 10 seconds the picture should go away
   and the page should say *Nothing to do*; the VPN should come up.
6. While the browser was open, the Status page should have said
   *RasBeacon's browser is open on the login page*.
7. **Report:**
   - did the login page appear? How long did it take?
   - could you log in? Anything that didn't work (a field you couldn't tap,
     a page that looked wrong, a pop-up, a terms checkbox)?
   - how did the phone feel: how many seconds between a tap and the
     picture changing?
   - the hotel's name/type (some use unusual login pages), and whether
     **Start login mode** was needed instead.
8. Leak check (if you can): on a laptop on the hotspot, before logging in,
   run `ping 8.8.8.8` and open a few websites. They must all **fail** the
   whole time until the VPN is back.

## 5. Things that must still work

1. Run the [milestone 4 plan](m4.md) steps 2 and 3 (kill switch) once.
2. **Report:** anything different from before.
